How it works

1

Every buy and sell pays a 5% tax. It lands in the launch's revenue contract on Argus, in USDC.

2

70% of it goes into the pot. The remaining 30% funds the project. It is stated here rather than hidden.

3

At the threshold, a draw block is announced. A block that has not been mined yet. Nobody can predict its hash — including us.

4

One holder takes the entire pot. Odds scale with the square root of your balance. Holding more helps, but doubling your bag multiplies your odds by 1.41 — not by 2. Whales cannot own every draw.

Why you can check it yourself

The winner comes from two public inputs: token balances frozen at the draw block, and the hash of that same block. Both are on-chain. Neither can be altered after the block is announced.

Every round below lists its draw block, the resulting hash and the payout transaction. Run the open-source verification script against any of them and you should land on the same winner.
