The house rules
I

Every trade pays a fee

It lands in the launch's fee contract on Pons. The launchpad takes its share first — what reaches this project is what is left after that.

II

70% of what arrives goes on the table

The other 30% funds the project. Written here rather than buried — you should know who takes what before you buy.

III

At the line, the wheel locks

A draw block is announced. It has not been mined yet, so its hash cannot be predicted — not by you, not by us.

IV

One address takes the pot

Odds follow the square root of your balance. Holding more helps; doubling your bag multiplies your odds by 1.41, not by 2. No single whale owns the wheel.

Why you don't have to trust us

The winner falls out of two public inputs: token balances frozen at the draw block, and the hash of that same block. Both live on Robinhood Chain. Neither can be touched once the block is announced.

Every spin below shows its draw block, the hash it produced and the payout transaction. The verification script is open source and imports the exact same module as the bot — point it at any round and you should land on the same address.
